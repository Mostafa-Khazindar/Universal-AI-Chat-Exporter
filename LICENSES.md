## Why Dual-Licensing (MIT / Apache 2.0)?

Dual-licensing under MIT and Apache 2.0 is the gold standard adopted by major software foundations (including the Rust language ecosystem, Python web frameworks, and Linux tools).

### Benefits for You (The Creator / Maintainer):
* **Ironclad Warranty Disclaimer:** Both licenses explicitly state the software is provided `AS IS`, without warranty of any kind. You cannot be held liable for any damages or account issues arising from using this software.
* **No Reverse Liability:** Users who download your code accept full responsibility for their own use of the software.
* **Patent Protection (Apache 2.0 Section 3):** Prevents contributors from asserting patent claims against your project.
* **Trademark Protection (Apache 2.0 Section 6):** Explicitly prohibits third parties from using your project’s name or brand to endorse downstream forks without permission.
